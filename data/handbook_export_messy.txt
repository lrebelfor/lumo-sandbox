About Lumo
Lumo is an online shop for design lamps, based in Utrecht,
the Netherlands.  We sell lamps from twelve European
designers, plus spare parts and LED bulbs.
Customer support is open Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00
(Dutch time).  Email support@lumo.example or use the chat
on our website.  We aim to reply within one working day; in
November and December this can take up to two working
days.
We do not have a physical shop.  Pick-up from our warehouse
in Utrecht is possible by appointment only, for orders
marked "ready for pick-up".
Ordering and payment
You can pay with iDEAL, Bancontact, credit card (Visa,
Mastercard, American Express), PayPal or Klarna (pay in 30
days).
Business customers with a VAT number can pay by invoice
within 14 days after their first three orders.
Prices include Dutch VAT (21%).  For business customers in
Belgium and Germany with a valid VAT number, we charge 0%
VAT (reverse charge).
You can change or cancel an order until it has the status

Shipping and delivery
We ship to the Netherlands, Belgium and Germany.  We do not
ship to other countries yet.
Orders placed before 15:00 on a working day leave our
warehouse the same day.  Orders placed later, or at
weekends, leave the next working day.
Delivery times: Netherlands 1-2 working days, Belgium 3-4
working days, Germany 2-4 working days.
Shipping is free for orders over EUR 75.  Below that: EUR
4.95 in the Netherlands, EUR 6.95 in Belgium and Germany.
Large lamps (floor lamps and pendants over 80 cm) ship
with DHL Freight on a pallet; delivery takes 4-7 working
days and the driver delivers to the ground floor only.
Small parcels ship with PostNL (Netherlands) or DHL
(Belgium, Germany).  You receive a tracking link by email
when your order ships.

Returns
You can return any item within 30 days of delivery, for
any reason.  Returns are free for customers in the
Netherlands and Belgium; customers in Germany pay EUR 6.95
return shipping.
The item must be unused and in its original packaging.
Lamps that have been installed by an electrician can still
be returned if they are undamaged.
Start a return through the returns page with your order

We refund within 14 days after we receive the return, to

Damaged or faulty items
Damaged on arrival: contact us within 7 days of delivery
with your order number and a photo of the damage and the
box.  We send a free replacement. You do not need to return
the broken item; please recycle it responsibly.
If the replacement is out of stock, you can choose a full
refund or a different lamp.
Faulty within warranty: see Warranty.  We may ask for a
short video showing the fault.
Never try to repair a lamp yourself while it is connected
to the mains.
Warranty
All lamps have a 2-year warranty from the delivery date.

LED modules that are built into a lamp have a 5-year
warranty.  Separate LED bulbs have a 1-year warranty.
The warranty covers manufacturing faults.  It does not
cover damage from falls, wrong installation, use outdoors
(unless the lamp is rated for outdoor use, IP44 or
higher), or normal wear of fabric shades.
For a warranty claim, email us your order number, a
description and a photo or video.  We repair the lamp, send
a replacement part, or replace the lamp.  If none of these
is possible, we refund the purchase price minus a
reasonable amount for use.
Products, bulbs and installation
Each product page lists the fitting (E27, E14 or GU10),
the maximum wattage, whether the lamp is dimmable, and the
IP rating.
Most of our table and floor lamps use an E27 fitting.  We
recommend our Lumo Warm LED bulbs (2700 K, 806 lumen).  Not
every LED bulb is dimmable: use dimmable bulbs with a
dimmer.
Pendant lamps must be installed by a qualified
electrician.  Table and floor lamps come with a plug and
need no installation.

Business customers
Businesses (cafés, offices, architects) get 10% off orders
over EUR 1,000 and a personal contact person.
To open a business account, email business@lumo.example
with your company name, VAT number and address.  Approval
takes up to 2 working days.
Volume quotes for more than 20 of the same lamp are
available within 3 working days.
Privacy and your data
We use your name, address, email and order history only to
process your order, provide support and, if you agreed,
send our newsletter.
We keep order data for 7 years because Dutch tax law
requires it.  Support emails are deleted after 2 years.
You can ask us for a copy of your data or ask us to delete
it (except what the law requires us to keep) by emailing
privacy@lumo.example.  We reply within 30 days.
We never sell your data.  Our payment providers and
carriers receive only the data they need.
