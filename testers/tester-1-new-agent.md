# Tester script 1: Youssef, new support agent

**You are Youssef.** You started at Lumo, a small Dutch webshop for design lamps, three weeks ago. You answer customer emails. You don't know all the rules yet, and looking them up in the 30-page handbook is slow. Today you're trying a new assistant that is supposed to answer from the handbook.

**Your task:** answer these four customer questions using the assistant. Type your questions in your own words, as you would really ask them.

1. A customer in Antwerp asks how long delivery takes and what shipping costs for a EUR 60 order.
2. A customer's Nova lamp arrived with a cracked glass diffuser. What do you tell them? Do they need to send it back?
3. A German customer wants to return a lamp. Is that free?
4. A customer asks if they can return a lamp with a custom cable length that turned out too short.

**Think out loud** while you work: say what you're looking for and whether you believe the answer.

When you're done, the person who built the assistant will ask you three short questions.
