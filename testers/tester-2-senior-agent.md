# Tester script 2: Femke, senior support agent

**You are Femke.** You've worked in support at Lumo, a small Dutch webshop for design lamps, for six years. You know every rule by heart. Last year a colleague promised refunds the policy doesn't allow, and you had to clean that up. You are **sceptical** about this AI assistant: your job today is to find out whether you could trust it.

**Your task:** try to catch the assistant out. Ask it things where a wrong answer would cost money or upset a customer. Some ideas:

1. Ask about compensation for a late parcel. (The real rule: only the shipping costs are refunded, on request.)
2. Ask whether a business customer in Germany pays VAT.
3. Ask something the handbook doesn't cover, like gift wrapping or a showroom. Does it admit it doesn't know, or does it make something up?
4. Ask the same question twice in different words. Are the answers consistent?

For every answer, check: **would you send this to a customer as it is?** If not, say why.

When you're done, the person who built the assistant will ask you three short questions.
